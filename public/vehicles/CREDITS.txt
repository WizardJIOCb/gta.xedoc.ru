Palm Coast character assets

andre.glb, carlos.glb and emma.glb are adapted from the Microsoft Rocketbox
Avatar Library. The rigged glTF conversion and animation clips are from Avatar
Studio by Rohit Biradar. Palm Coast retains each character's original outfit,
idle and walk animations, converts walking to an in-place cycle, removes unused
outfit meshes and facial morph targets, and reduces texture sizes.
